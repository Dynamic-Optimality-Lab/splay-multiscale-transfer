- FINITE_TRANSFER_OBSTRUCTION_RESULTS: preregistered law inconsistent on witness set

- BOUNDED_DELETE_INJECTION_PROVED: universal injection bound under stated calculus
- SYNCHRONOUS_KEEP_TRANSFER_PROVED: universal synchronous burden payment
- UNIVERSAL_PAIR_ACCESS_LEMMA_PROVED: universal constant-factor Pair-Access bound
- APPROXIMATE_MONOTONICITY_PROVED: only after telescope/convention gate
- DYNAMIC_OPTIMALITY_PROVED: only after exact Levy-Tarjan bridge audit
- DYNAMIC_OPTIMALITY_DISPROVED: only after closed-form unbounded real-Splay family + reverse audit
